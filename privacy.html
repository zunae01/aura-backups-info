Aura Backups

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Purpose

Aura Backups is a private, personal backup utility operated solely by its owner. It is not offered to the public and does not provide user accounts.

Google Drive access

The utility accesses the owner’s Google Drive only to create, verify, organize, and manage backup files requested by the owner. Its access is limited to the owner’s own Google account.

Data handling

Aura Backups does not collect, sell, share, or disclose personal information to third parties. It uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-processing services. Backup data and OAuth credentials remain under the owner’s control.

Retention and removal

Backup files remain in the owner’s Google Drive until the owner removes them. Google Drive access can be revoked at any time from the owner’s Google Account security settings.
